# Privacy Policy — Popsicle Panic

**Last updated: 2026-06-16**

## 1. Who We Are

Popsicle Panic is developed and published by an independent developer. Contact: brmgloth.entertainment@gmail.com

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Data You Provide
We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

### 2.2 Data Collected Automatically

| Category | What | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Firebase Analytics | Anonymized gameplay events (level start/complete, session length) | Understand how the game is played and improve balance |
| Firebase Crashlytics | Crash reports, device model, OS version | Fix bugs |
| Firebase App Check | Device integrity token | Prevent abuse of cloud services |

All Firebase services are provided by Google LLC. Firebase data is processed under [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### 2.3 Data We Do NOT Collect
- Name, email address, or any contact details
- Location data
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID) — no ad SDKs are integrated
- Contacts, camera, microphone, or other sensitive device permissions
- Financial information (in-app purchase transactions are processed entirely by the App Store / Google Play)

## 3. How We Use Data

- **Gameplay analytics** — to tune level difficulty and pacing (Firebase Analytics).
- **Crash reporting** — to identify and fix technical issues.
- **No marketing, no profiling, no third-party advertising.**

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with third parties, except:
- **Google (Firebase)** as our backend provider, under a data processing agreement.
- **Apple App Store / Google Play** for purchase verification (we receive only a transaction receipt, not payment details).
- **Legal obligations** — if required by law.

## 5. Data Retention

Firebase Analytics retains event data for **2 months** (default retention window). Crash logs are retained for **90 days**. We do not store any persistent user profiles on our servers.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Popsicle Panic is rated for ages 4 and up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not include behavioral advertising or social features. If you believe your child has provided personal data, contact us at brmgloth.entertainment@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete data associated with your device. Since we do not collect personally identifiable information, most requests can be fulfilled by:
- Deleting the app (removes all local save data).
- Resetting your Firebase Analytics identifier in your device's privacy settings.

For any privacy requests, contact: **brmgloth.entertainment@gmail.com**

## 8. Security

Data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted in transit (TLS). We follow Firebase's recommended security rules to restrict cloud access.

## 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this document will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the game after changes constitutes acceptance.

## 10. Contact

Questions or requests regarding privacy: **brmgloth.entertainment@gmail.com**
